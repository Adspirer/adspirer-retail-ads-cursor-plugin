## Longer description

Amazon & Walmart retail advertising, reporting, and optimization inside Cursor. Adspirer Retail Ads connects Amazon Ads and Walmart Connect so you can analyze campaign performance, measure advertising cost of sales (ACoS) and return on ad spend (ROAS), investigate keyword and product performance, and identify opportunities to improve budgets and bids.

Move from insight to action: research keywords, plan campaigns, and create or optimize supported campaigns with your approval. The plugin includes 11 retail advertising skills, a specialist agent, and a hosted MCP connection with browser-based OAuth. An Adspirer account and connected advertising accounts are required; platform permissions, eligibility, and service-plan limits apply.
